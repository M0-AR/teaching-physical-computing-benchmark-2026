## 1 Introduction
Physical computing (micro:bit/Arduino/Pi) + PBL promises constructionist, real-world CS education, but teacher programs are rarely audited reproducibly. ArmEducationX's 42-week certificate is an ideal instrument: fixed syllabus, IPO scaffolding, staged hardware, explicit assessment + soft-skills strands.

## 6 Discussion — the six patterns as theory
H1 IPO as DSL; H2 formative inversion; H3 developmental hardware gradient; H4 collaboration-as-debugging; H5 context primacy (SDGs); H6 efficacy mediation model: Adoption = f(efficacy, support) ≫ g(device specs). Each with falsifier + proposed RCT/quasi-experiment, instrument (IPO sheet, CT-MCQ bank, debug-observation rubric), and power notes (class-size guidance per Guerzhoy 2023 medium-n).
